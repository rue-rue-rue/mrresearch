01 / Architecture

A dedicated loop for research

The host agent coordinates the task. The research subagent searches, reads, and follows up on gaps, then returns its findings and sources for the answer or report.

02 / Method

Carry the evidence through

Each stage preserves the sources and context needed for the next, from discovering candidate pages to writing a claim.

01 Search for missing information

Form distinct queries for the topic, primary sources, and items to check. Normalize, deduplicate, and rank candidate links to decide which pages to read.

02 Read sources and locate passages

Read relevant pages and prioritize primary reports, disclosures, and direct data. Refine the target passage or seek other sources when a page offers limited evidence.

03 Carry source details with findings

Pass research findings along with available page titles, site details, and original URLs so readers can trace important claims to their sources.

04 Preserve time and scope in the report

Retain the entity, figures, units, dates, and definitions during synthesis. Distinguish sourced facts, analysis, and unresolved gaps.

03 / Example question

How does long-duration energy storage move beyond pilots?

Explore technology, deployment, and policy as separate lines of inquiry, then synthesize the conditions for adoption and the remaining uncertainties.
